When toilets gurgle, the basement floor drain bubbles and tubs fill from below, you need emergency sewer line cleaning in Hillside. Stop all water use and call the 24/7 line. We clear the main with heavy equipment and tell you what we found.

A main line clog is different from a sink clog. It affects every fixture, it can put sewage on a floor and it often has a cause that will return unless it is fixed.

Signs your main line needs cleaning

  • Toilets gurgle when the washer drains.
  • Several fixtures drain slowly at once.
  • A floor drain bubbles or smells.
  • Sewage backs up in the lowest tub or shower.
  • Backups that come back every few months.

How we clean a main line

  1. Find the cleanoutWe locate or add a cleanout so we do not have to work through a toilet.
  2. Run the cableA heavy cable with a cutter head breaks through the blockage and cuts roots.
  3. Flush and testWe flush the line and run water to confirm it flows freely.
  4. Camera checkWe look at the pipe to find the cause, so the same backup does not return.

Cable machine or hydro jet

A cable clears a blockage fast and is the right first move in an emergency. If the line is coated with grease or scale, jetting follows. Hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall and keeps the line clear longer. We pick the tool based on what the line needs, not what is on the truck.

Why Hillside main lines clog

Union County's township profile says more than 79 percent of Hillside homes were built before 1960. Sewer pipe from that period is often clay or cast iron, and both snag paper and grease as they age. Two-family buildings send more flow down a single lateral, so a partial clog fills faster.

Tree roots are the other common cause. Roots find the small gaps at clay joints and fill the pipe over a few seasons. Cleaning removes them, and a repair stops them returning.

What to do while you wait

  • Stop using every water fixture.
  • Keep the lowest floor clear and people away from standing water.
  • Do not pour chemical cleaners into a main line.
  • Photograph the backup for your insurer.

Preventing another backup

A cleaned line stays clear only if the cause is addressed. Roots need a repair. Grease needs a change in kitchen habits and sometimes regular jetting. Flushed wipes need to stop. Ask the plumber for the camera footage so you can see what caused it and decide on the right fix.

Cleanouts: the cheapest upgrade

A cleanout is a capped pipe that gives direct access to the sewer line. Houses without one force plumbers to work through a toilet, which takes longer and risks the fixture. Adding a cleanout makes future cleaning and inspections faster and less messy.

  • Cable only, or jetting too.
  • Whether a cleanout must be installed.
