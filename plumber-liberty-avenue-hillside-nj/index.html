Who is responsible when several tenants share a line

In a building with more than one tenant, the first question is who owns which pipe. A drain that serves the whole building is often the landlord's responsibility, while a fixture inside one unit is often the tenant's. Your lease decides. We tell you what we find and where the problem sits, and we leave the lease question to you and the owner.

If you manage the building, tell us who can approve work and who has keys. Many calls come at night when the shop is closed, and a plumber who cannot get in cannot help.

Grease and backflow: what to ask about

Restaurants commonly have a grease trap or interceptor that needs regular cleaning, and many commercial water systems have backflow preventers that need periodic testing. The schedule depends on the building and on local code. Ask the Hillside Building and Housing Department what applies to your address, then call us for the plumbing side of the work. We clear kitchen drain lines with hydro jetting and can tell you honestly when a trap needs a specialist hauler.

Rain and low spots near Hillside Avenue

The 2009 Hillside master plan lists Highland Avenue and Hillside Avenue among places that flooded in small rain events when the plan was written, and it points to undersized culverts and added paving as causes. If your shop has a floor drain or a below-grade storage room, keep the drain clear and test any sump or ejector pump before a storm.
