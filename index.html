What to do before the plumber arrives

Start with the water. Turn the main shutoff clockwise until it stops, then open the lowest faucet in the house to drain the lines. If water is near the electrical panel or a power strip, stay out of the room and say so on the call.

If the water heater is involved, turn the gas control to pilot or switch the breaker off before you touch anything. Move rugs, cardboard and electronics out of the wet zone, and photograph the damage before cleanup because your insurer will ask for it.

For a sewer backup, stop running water in the house. Every flush and every load of laundry sends more water toward the same blocked line.
