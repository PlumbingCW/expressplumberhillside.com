Privacy Policy

How Hillside Express Plumbing handles your information.

☎ Call (908) 357-1542

Last updated November 16, 2026. This policy explains what information Hillside Express Plumbing, 490 Hillside Ave, Hillside, NJ 07205, collects when you visit expressplumberhillside.com or call (908) 357-1542.

Information we collect

When you call, we ask for your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem so we can schedule a visit. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you email us, we receive your email address and the message you send. We do not use web forms.

Website data

Like most websites, our web host may log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. We use it to keep the site working and secure.

How we use information

  • To schedule and complete plumbing work.
  • To answer questions and follow up on a job.
  • To improve our service and train our staff.
  • To meet legal and insurance requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service partners who help us complete your job, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us not to record a call at the start of the call. You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing contact@expressplumberhillside.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@expressplumberhillside.com or call (908) 357-1542. See also our terms of use.
