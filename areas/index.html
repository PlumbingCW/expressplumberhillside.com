Hillside, NJHome base.

We are based at 490 Hillside Avenue in Hillside, Union County, and our trucks start from there. Hillside borders Irvington, Elizabeth and Newark, so most calls are a short drive away. Maplewood is a little farther, but still close enough for fast response. Each town below has its own page, built around what that town actually calls about.

How we cover the area

Dispatch is local. The nearest available plumber takes the call, and the dispatcher gives you an honest arrival window based on traffic and the day's jobs. We do not claim to reach every part of a large city. In Newark we serve the South Ward and the blocks along the Hillside line, including Weequahic.

Neighborhoods and corridors inside Hillside

Inside Hillside we have pages for Westminster, a residential section near Conant Park, for the business blocks along Liberty Avenue and for the Route 22 commercial corridor. Hillside has few formally named neighborhoods, so we only list places with a real local identity.

Hillside itself

Hillside covers about 2.78 square miles in Union County, and more than 79 percent of its homes were built before 1960, according to the county's township profile. That means old drains, old supply lines and old boilers. Our service pages explain what we do about each. The township's Building and Housing Department can be reached at (973) 926-5100 for permit questions.

Standards and sources behind this page

Plumbing work in each of these towns follows the same state code, according to the New Jersey Division of Codes and Standards. Each town's building office handles its own permits.
