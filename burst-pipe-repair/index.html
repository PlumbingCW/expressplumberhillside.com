A burst pipe can release dozens of gallons a minute, so emergency burst pipe repair in Hillside starts with the shutoff, not the phone call. Close the main water valve now, open a faucet on the lowest floor, then call our 24/7 line. A plumber will be sent the same day when one is available.

Once the water is off, the damage stops growing. What you do in the next few minutes decides whether you have a repair or a renovation.

When a burst pipe is an emergency

If water is flowing, treat it as an emergency. These steps buy time until the plumber arrives.

Close the main valveTurn it clockwise until it stops. It is usually where the water line enters the basement.
Drain the linesOpen the lowest faucet and flush a toilet to relieve pressure and empty the pipes.
Kill power to wet areasOnly if you can reach the panel from a dry spot. Never stand in water near electrical.
Protect what you canMove rugs, boxes and electronics, and photograph the damage for your insurer.

Why pipes burst in Hillside

New Jersey winters bring hard freezes. Water expands as it freezes and the pipe splits where the ice builds pressure, often hours later when the ice thaws. Pipes on exterior walls, in unheated basements and in garages are the usual victims. The second cause is age. Many Hillside homes were built before 1960, and old galvanized steel corrodes from the inside until a pinhole becomes a split.

A third cause is a failed fitting or a hose bib line left connected through winter. The pipe may look fine until the first cold night.

What the repair looks like

  1. Stop the waterWe confirm the shutoff works or close the water at the curb box.
  2. Find the damageWe check beyond the split for more damage, since a freeze often cracks more than one spot.
  3. Cut and replaceThe damaged section is cut out and replaced with copper or PEX and proper fittings.
  4. Test under pressureWe restore the water, check every joint and watch for drips.
  5. Advise on dryingWe tell you what to dry and who to call if walls or floors are wet.

Frozen pipes that have not burst yet

A pipe that is frozen but not yet split is a warning. Open the faucet slightly, warm the pipe with a hair dryer starting near the faucet end, and never use a flame. If you cannot find the frozen spot or water is already leaking, call. A plumber can thaw the line safely and check it for cracks before you turn it back on.

After a cold night, check pipes along north-facing walls and in garages. Prevention is cheap. Insulate exposed lines, disconnect hoses from outdoor bibs, and open cabinet doors on exterior walls during a deep freeze.

Repair versus repipe

One burst on a young copper line is a repair. A third burst on old galvanized pipe is a message that the whole run is tired. We will tell you if the pattern points to repiping, and we will say so even when a single repair would earn us less. If you are not sure where the water is coming from, leak detection can locate it before any walls are opened.

Water damage after a burst pipe

We repair the pipe. Drying walls, pulling wet insulation and removing flooring is a separate job for a restoration company. Wet drywall and carpet should come out within a day or two to limit mold, so line up that help while the plumber works.

Standards and sources behind this page

Work described here falls under New Jersey's Uniform Construction Code, whose plumbing subcode is the National Standard Plumbing Code, 2021 New Jersey edition, according to the New Jersey Division of Codes and Standards.
