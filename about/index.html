About Hillside Express Plumbing

A local plumbing team on Hillside Avenue with a 24/7 line and a simple promise on price.

Hillside Express Plumbing is based at 490 Hillside Avenue in Hillside, New Jersey. We take emergency calls around the clock and do planned work during the day. The business is built around three habits: answer the phone, say the price before work starts and explain the cause so you can decide.

How we work

  1. You callThe dispatcher asks what is happening and tells you what to do right away, such as closing a valve.
  2. We scheduleA plumber is scheduled for the same day when one is available.
  3. We diagnoseThe plumber finds the cause, explains it and gives you the price.
  4. We fix itWork starts after you agree. We test the repair and show you what was done.

What we stand behind

  • A clear price before work starts.
  • Same-day appointments when available.
  • Licensed and insured plumbers.
  • Serving Hillside and nearby towns.

What we do not do

We do not invent urgency. If a repair can wait for a daytime visit, we say so. If a part is cheaper to repair than replace, we say that too. We do not sell products you do not need, and we are open about the limits of a repair.

Standards we follow

Plumbing work in New Jersey is governed by state code and enforced by local building departments. In Hillside the Building and Housing Department handles permits and inspections and can be reached at (973) 926-5100. We follow the code and arrange inspections where they are required.

Our local focus

Hillside is a township of about 2.78 square miles in Union County. Most of its homes are older, which shapes our work: cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines, basement boilers and sump pumps. Nearby, we serve Irvington, Elizabeth, Newark's South Ward and Maplewood. See all plumbing services we offer.

What a first call sounds like

The dispatcher asks four things: where you are, what is happening, whether water or sewage is spreading and whether anyone is at risk. If water is escaping, you hear how to close the main valve before anything else. If you smell gas, you are told to leave and call the gas utility or 911 first. Only then do we talk about scheduling.

We give an arrival window based on the day's jobs and the traffic, not a promise we cannot keep. If a plumber is delayed, we call you.

How we handle price

You hear the price before work starts. That price is based on what the plumber finds, written in plain terms and tied to a specific repair. If the plumber finds a second problem, we stop and explain it before adding anything. After hours and holiday visits cost more, and we say so on the phone, not after the job.

Two-family homes and rentals

Many homes in Hillside and the towns around it hold more than one household. We work with owners, tenants and property managers. A landlord can approve a repair by phone, and a tenant can call us about active water. We explain the cause to both sides so the repair is understood and the same problem does not come back.

Where we are less useful

We are a plumbing company. We repair pipes, drains, fixtures, water heaters, boilers and pumps. We do not dry out walls, rebuild drywall or remove mold, and we do not pretend to. When your job needs another trade, we say so and tell you what to ask them for.

Knowing the ground we work on

Hillside is crossed by Interstate 78, U.S. Route 22, the Garden State Parkway and Route 439, and it includes the localities of Lyons Farms, Westminster and Saybrooke. Conant Park, the township's largest park, anchors its streets. Knowing that map matters on an emergency call, because a dispatcher who can picture Liberty Avenue or Hillside Avenue can give you an honest arrival window.

Why we answer the phone ourselves

A plumbing emergency is stressful, and the first voice you hear sets the tone. Our line is staffed around the clock by people who know what a burst pipe or a backed up main looks like. They do not read a script. They ask what you see, tell you what to do and send the nearest plumber. That is the whole idea behind the name Express: less waiting, less guessing.

After the job, we keep a record of what was found and what was done. If something does not hold, call us and we will look at it again.
