Winter in Maplewood: freezes and bursts

Maplewood has a lot of older houses, and older houses lose heat through exterior walls where pipes sit. After a hard freeze, the first sign is often no water from one faucet. Open it slightly and warm the pipe slowly. If a pipe has split, the thaw brings the flood. Burst pipe repair begins with the main valve.

Winter is also when boilers and water heaters fail. An older house with a steam or hot water boiler needs it serviced before the cold. See boiler repair.

Spring and storm season: basements and sewers

Heavy spring rain tests sump pumps and floor drains. Maplewood sits at the foot of South Mountain Reservation, a 2,100 acre park to the northwest, and its streets slope toward lower ground. A basement that stays dry for most of the year can fill in one storm if the pump fails. We test and replace pumps through sump pump service.

Summer and fall: slow leaks and tree roots

Maplewood is known for large trees and mature yards. Roots find the joints in old clay or cast iron sewer lines, and a slow backup in summer can become a full one in fall leaf season. A camera inspection shows root intrusion before it stops the line. Drain cleaning handles the clog, and a repair fixes the cause.

What the age of Maplewood homes means

More than half of Maplewood's housing units, about 55 percent, were built in 1939 or earlier, and about 71 percent before 1960, per Census Bureau American Community Survey figures. Edward Balch built 176 homes here starting around 1900. Houses of that period often carry galvanized supply pipe, cast iron drains and original fixtures.

Maplewood Village, the downtown on Springfield Avenue, has shops and restaurants whose lines need regular care. Hilton and Jefferson Village are residential neighborhoods where the same age patterns apply. We also serve South Orange, which borders Maplewood to the north.

Water and permits

New Jersey American Water serves Maplewood. Permit rules for plumbing work come from the township, so ask before a larger job.

Protecting old finishes

Maplewood homes often have plaster walls, hardwood floors and older tile. We lay protection down before we work, open walls only where needed and discuss access points with you in advance. If a wall has to be opened, we tell you where, how large and who can patch it.

If you are planning a renovation, tell us before the work starts. A bathroom remodel in a house this age is the best time to replace worn supply and drain lines, since the wall is already open.
