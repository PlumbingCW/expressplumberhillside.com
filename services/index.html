Hillside Express Plumbing handles the full range of residential and light commercial plumbing in Hillside, NJ. Some calls are emergencies, like a burst pipe at midnight or a sewer backup. Others are planned, like a new water heater or a bathroom remodel. Every service below has its own page that explains what happens, what affects the price and what questions to ask.

Emergency plumbing services

These are the problems where waiting makes damage worse. Our 24/7 line takes calls at any hour. We tell you what to do while a plumber is on the way, and we schedule a same-day visit when one is available. The emergency group includes burst pipe repair, drain cleaning, sewer line repair, water heater repair, sump pump repair and leak detection.

Planned work and inspections

Installs, upgrades and inspections can be scheduled around your day. This group includes water heater installation, tankless water heaters, repiping, plumbing installation, sewer camera inspection, basement waterproofing and boiler repair. Planned work often costs less than the emergency it prevents, which is why we encourage a camera inspection or a water heater check before something fails.
