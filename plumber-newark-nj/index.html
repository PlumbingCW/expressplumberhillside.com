What housing in the South Ward means for your pipes

Newark's housing is a mix. About 43 percent of units were built before 1960 and the median year built is 1966, per Census Bureau American Community Survey figures. Weequahic was first built out with detached single-family homes around Weequahic Park, and high-rise apartment towers rose along Elizabeth Avenue in the 1960s and 1970s.

Those two housing types call for plumbers in different ways. A pre-1960 house has older cast iron, galvanized supply and a basement boiler. A mid-century tower has long vertical risers, shared hot water systems and building-wide shutoffs. If you live in a tower, call your building manager as well. For a house, call us.

Weequahic Park and the surrounding blocks

Weequahic Park, a 311 acre Olmsted Brothers design with an 80 acre lake, anchors the neighborhood. The homes around it were built to a high standard in their day and many are well kept. Well kept does not mean new pipe. A house that has had its kitchen redone can still have a 1940s main drain. We check what the new work connects to, and recommend a camera inspection before a purchase.

Hawthorne Avenue marks the neighborhood's northern edge, and Irvington lies to the west. Newark Liberty Airport is to the east, and Hillside and Elizabeth are to the south, which is why a plumber from Hillside is close.

Heat and hot water through winter

Boilers and water heaters do most of their work between November and March, and that is when they fail. If your heat is out, see boiler repair. A burst line after a hard freeze is a burst pipe call.

Permits and utilities

Plumbing permits in Newark come from the city's construction code office, and we recommend asking them before larger work. We do not assume the details of your water supply, so ask the water provider listed on your bill.

Working with property managers in the South Ward

Many South Ward addresses are managed buildings. If you are a tenant, tell your manager when you call us. If you are the manager, give us the contact for the person who approves work. This avoids delays, since a plumber cannot start on a shared line without approval.

For single family owners around Weequahic Park, the questions are different. How old is the water heater, what is the main drain made of and has the house had a sewer inspection? We can answer those on the first visit.
